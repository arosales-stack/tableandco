Amberidge Collection N°1

Wedgwood Columbia, made in England. Amber griffins and scrolls frame hand-enamelled sprays of roses, peonies and forget-me-nots, with a matching bouquet at the centre of every plate. The design was first drawn by Wedgwood’s Thomas Allen in the 1880s.

12 place settings
Time period1950-1960
Place settings12
Best forRehearsal dinners, bridal showers, anniversaries, Christmas dinners, formal dinner parties
StyleWedgwood Columbia (enamelled), amber griffins & hand-coloured florals

Holds its own with crystal and silver at a formal dinner. Keep the linens plain and let the plate carry the colour.
